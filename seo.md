# SEO

This page separates what the theme does automatically from what you need to do yourself.

**No theme can guarantee search rankings.** Rankings depend on your products, your content, your
competitors and Google's judgment. What a theme can do is make sure nothing technical stands in
the way — and that is what this one does.

---

## What the theme provides

You do not need to configure any of this. It is built in.

### Page titles

Every page has a `<title>` built from the page's own title and your store name, plus:

- The tag name, on tag-filtered listings
- The page number, on paginated listings

This means page 2 of a collection does not compete with page 1 for the same title.

### Meta descriptions

The theme outputs a meta description on every page that has one. **The text comes from you** —
see [What you are responsible for](#what-you-are-responsible-for).

### Canonical URLs

Every page declares its canonical URL, so search engines know which version of a URL is the real
one. This matters on filtered and sorted collection pages, which produce many URLs for the same
content.

### Pagination hints

Paginated pages declare `prev` and `next` links, so search engines understand they are a sequence
rather than duplicates.

### Heading structure

Each page has one main heading, with subheadings nested logically beneath it. Section headings
use the correct levels rather than being styled to look like headings.

Some sections offer a **Heading size** setting — this changes the visual size only. The
underlying structure stays correct, so you can make a heading look smaller without breaking the
page's outline.

### Structured data

The theme emits structured data (schema.org JSON-LD) that helps search engines understand your
pages:

| Type | Where | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product** | Product pages | Generated by Shopify from your product data — name, description, images, price, availability, SKU. |
| **BreadcrumbList** | Product, collection, collections list, blog, article, page and search pages | The navigation trail to that page. |
| **BlogPosting** | Article pages | Headline, publish and update dates, image, description, author, publisher. |
| **WebSite** with search action | Home page | Lets search engines offer a search box for your site in results. |
| **Organization** | Every page | Your business name, URL, logo and social profiles. Controlled by **Theme settings → SEO and sharing → Enable organization schema**. |

Structured data is generated from your Shopify data. Accurate product data produces accurate
structured data; incomplete product data produces incomplete structured data.

### Social sharing metadata

Every page carries **Open Graph** and **Twitter Card** tags, so a shared link shows a proper
preview on Facebook, LinkedIn, X, WhatsApp, Slack and similar:

- Title, description, URL, site name and locale
- An image — the page's own image where it has one, falling back to your **Social sharing image**
- Image dimensions and alt text, so the preview renders correctly
- `og:type` set appropriately per page (product, article, website)
- Price and currency on product pages

**Set a social sharing image** under **Theme settings → SEO and sharing**. 1200 × 628px
recommended. Without one, a shared home page link may show no image at all.

### Image alt text

The theme outputs the alt text you set in Shopify admin. Where an image has none, it falls back
sensibly — a product image uses the product title, a logo uses your store name.

Fallbacks are a safety net, not a substitute. Write real alt text.

### Semantic, crawlable markup

Content is rendered as real HTML text, not built by JavaScript, so search engines see the same
page a visitor does. Navigation menus, product information, prices and descriptions are all
present in the initial page.

### Fast, correctly sized images

Responsive images and lazy loading are built in. Page speed is a ranking factor, and image weight
is the largest single contributor to it on most stores. See [Performance](performance.md).

### robots.txt

Shopify serves `robots.txt` for you, and it already tells search engines which URLs to ignore —
checkout, cart, and internal search results. The theme does not override it. If you need to change
a rule, add a `templates/robots.txt.liquid` file to your own copy of the theme; see
[Editing robots.txt.liquid](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo/editing-robots-txt).

## What you are responsible for

The theme cannot write your content. These are the highest-value things you can do.

### Page titles and meta descriptions

Every product, collection, page and blog post in Shopify admin has a **Search engine listing**
section — scroll to the bottom of the edit screen and click **Edit**.

| Field | Guidance |
| --- | --- |
| **Page title** | 50–60 characters. Lead with what the thing is. Include your brand if there is room. |
| **Meta description** | 140–160 characters. Describe the page and give a reason to click. Not a keyword list. |

Shopify generates these from your content if you leave them blank, which is workable — but a
written one almost always performs better. Prioritize your top products and collections.

### URLs

Also in the **Search engine listing** section. Shopify generates a URL handle from the title.

- Keep them short and readable: `/products/merino-crew-sweater`
- Use hyphens, lowercase, no dates or IDs
- **Changing a URL breaks existing links.** Shopify offers to create a redirect when you change
  one — always accept it.

### Alt text on images

Set it in Shopify admin by clicking an image and choosing **Add alt text**. Describe what the
image shows. See [Images and media](images-and-media.md).

### Product content

The single biggest SEO factor on a store is the quality of the product data.

- **Unique descriptions.** Manufacturer text that appears on a hundred other sites will not rank.
- **Complete information.** Materials, dimensions, care, compatibility, what is in the box.
- **The words customers use.** Search for your own products the way a customer would, and check
  those words appear.

### Collection descriptions

Collections rank well because they match how people search ("men's merino sweaters"). Give each
important collection a real description, not a single sentence.

### Content

A blog, buying guides, size guides and FAQ pages give search engines something to index and
customers a reason to trust you. This is slow work and it compounds.

### Redirects

When you delete or rename a product, page or collection, set up a redirect under
**Shopify admin → Content → Menus → URL redirects**. Otherwise the old link becomes a 404 —
losing whatever ranking and inbound links it had.

### Submit and monitor

- Verify your store in **Google Search Console** and submit your sitemap
  (`your-store.com/sitemap.xml`, generated by Shopify).
- Watch the Coverage and Performance reports for pages that are not indexed or not getting clicks.

## What no theme can do

- **Guarantee rankings.** Anyone who promises this is selling something.
- **Write your content.**
- **Fix thin or duplicated product descriptions.**
- **Earn links to your store.**
- **Substitute for a good product.**

The theme's job is to give your content the cleanest possible technical footing. The rest is the
content itself.
